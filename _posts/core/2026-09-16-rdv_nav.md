# The Basics of Navigating Randovania

Download, install, and open the latest version of Randovania.
Navigate to the Games tab and click a game.
{% include blog/figure.html src="/assets/guides/rdv_nav/rdv1.png" caption="The Games tab of Randovania, showing multiple games to choose from." %}

If you are new to randomizers or want something simple to begin with, it is recommended to click the “Quick Generate” button to generate a seed with the game’s recommended default setting.
{% include blog/figure.html src="/assets/guides/rdv_nav/rdv2.png" caption="The Games tab of Randovania, showing multiple games to choose from." %}

An example of a game’s Introduction tab. Quick Generate (Starter Preset) is highlighted.
Alternatively, you can navigate to the “Play” tab and generate your randomized game from a preset on the left, or even create a new one that suits your needs.

Every game has its own suite of settings for you to mix and match. What you enable is up to personal preference. In addition, you can right-click a preset to make any changes you desire.
{% include blog/figure.html src="/assets/guides/rdv_nav/rdv3.png" caption="An example of a game’s Play tab. On the left is the list of your presets, and on the right are the selected presets details." %}

An example of a game’s Play tab. On the left is a list of your presets, and on the right are the selected presets details.
Once you have generated a game with your chosen preset, the Game Details window will appear.
{% include blog/figure.html src="/assets/guides/rdv_nav/rdv4.png" caption="The Game Details window, with “Export Game” highlighted." %}

Click “Export Game” to open another window, in which the process differs from game to game.
Once the requirements are met, press “Accept”.
